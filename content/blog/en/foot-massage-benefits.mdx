## What Is Foot Reflexology?

Foot reflexology is rooted in Traditional Chinese Medicine. By stimulating reflex zones on the soles of the feet, therapists can regulate the function of corresponding organs throughout the body.

## Five Key Benefits

### 1. Improved Blood Circulation

Prolonged standing or sitting leads to poor circulation in the lower limbs. Foot massage effectively promotes blood flow, reducing heaviness and swelling in the legs.

### 2. Stress and Anxiety Relief

During a session, the body releases endorphins that help calm the nervous system. Many clients report feeling deeply relaxed after treatment.

### 3. Better Sleep Quality

Regular foot reflexology helps regulate the autonomic nervous system, improving insomnia. For best results, schedule your session a few hours before bedtime.

### 4. Headache and Migraine Relief

Specific reflex zones on the feet correspond to the head. Precise pressure techniques can effectively ease tension headaches and migraines.

### 5. Stronger Immune System

Studies show that regular massage boosts white blood cell activity, strengthening the body's natural defences and reducing the frequency of colds and illness.
