## Why the Lymphatic System Matters

The lymphatic system is a vital part of your immune defence. It transports lymph fluid, filters waste, and fights pathogens. When lymph circulation slows down, the body becomes prone to swelling, fatigue, and weakened immunity.

## What Is Lymphatic Drainage Massage?

Lymphatic drainage massage uses gentle, rhythmic strokes that follow the natural path of the lymph vessels. This helps guide lymph fluid movement and accelerates the removal of waste and excess fluid from the body.

Unlike deep tissue massage, lymphatic drainage uses lighter pressure — but the results run deep.

## Who Benefits Most?

- People who experience frequent swelling or water retention
- Office workers who sit for long hours
- Those with weakened immunity or frequent colds
- Post-surgery recovery patients (with doctor's approval)
- Anyone looking to improve skin tone and complexion

## What to Expect After a Session

Most clients feel noticeably lighter after their first session. With regular treatments, swelling reduces significantly and overall energy levels improve.

## Tips to Keep in Mind

- Drink plenty of water before and after your session to help flush toxins
- Avoid scheduling immediately after a heavy meal or on an empty stomach
- Consult your doctor first if you have any serious medical conditions
